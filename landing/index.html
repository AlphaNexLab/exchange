The Trade Flow

How Your Trade Settles

Step 1 of 3

You initiate a trade

Connect your Nautilus wallet and select your trading pair. Submit your order — no intermediary, no sign-up, no KYC.

Step 2 of 3

Smart contract locks funds in eUTXO

Your funds are locked into an ErgoScript smart contract using the extended UTXO model. Every condition is transparent and verifiable on-chain.

Step 3 of 3

Atomic swap executes on-chain

The trade resolves atomically — both parties receive their assets simultaneously, or the transaction is reverted. Zero counterparty risk.
